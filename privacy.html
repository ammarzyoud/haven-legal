3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 When you request a property viewing

To arrange a viewing we ask for and store:

  • your full name
  • your phone number
  • your email address
  • your preferred viewing date and time slot
  • the property you want to view
  • any notes you choose to add

We need this to contact you, confirm the appointment, and give the assigned agent enough information to meet you.

3.2 When you use the chat assistant on our website

Our website includes a chat assistant that answers questions about listings. If you ask to be contacted or to book a viewing, it will ask for your name and a phone number or email address so an agent can follow up. You are not required to provide these to browse or to ask questions.

[DISCLOSE IF APPLICABLE: Conversations with the chat assistant are stored so we can follow up on enquiries and improve our answers. State the retention period in section 7.]

3.3 When you contact an agent directly

Our listings offer links to call or message an agent on WhatsApp. If you use these, your phone number and message become visible to that agent. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy — we receive only what you send to the agent.

3.4 If you are a property owner with a Haven account

Property owners who list with us have accounts in our administration system. For these we store:

  • name and email address
  • a cryptographically hashed password (we never store or see the password itself)
  • the listings you create and their content and photographs
  • records of advertising campaigns created for your listings

3.5 If you connect a Meta (Facebook) account

Property owners may optionally connect their own Facebook Page and Meta advertising account so that Haven can prepare advertisements for their listings. If you do this, we store:

  • your Meta account ID and display name
  • the list of Facebook Pages and advertising accounts you administer
  • access tokens issued to us by Meta, and their expiry dates
  • the Page and advertising account you select

Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are excluded from our system's data exports and logs. They allow Haven to create advertisements in your advertising account. Haven creates all advertisements in a PAUSED state; no advertising spend occurs unless you activate the advertisement yourself in Meta Ads Manager. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored tokens.

3.6 Technical and session data

When you sign in to the Haven administration area we set a session cookie that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Our web servers keep standard access logs, which may include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

[DISCLOSE IF APPLICABLE: If you add analytics, advertising pixels, or any non-essential cookies, they must be listed here and must have consent before they load. At the time of writing Haven uses no analytics or advertising cookies on its public website.]

3.7 Data we do not collect

Haven does not collect or process payment card details, bank account details, national identity numbers, or passport numbers through this platform. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Automated Content Generation

Haven uses an artificial intelligence service provided by OpenAI to draft marketing material for property listings — descriptions, social media posts, advertisement copy, video scripts, and email and messaging drafts.

What is sent to OpenAI is information about the property itself: its name, location, price, size, number of rooms, features and similar details, as supplied by the property owner.

We do not send client personal data — names, phone numbers, email addresses, or viewing requests — to OpenAI.

The resulting text is reviewed inside Haven before it is used. No decision affecting you personally is made by automated means.

6. Who We Share Data With

We share personal data only as follows:

  • Haven agents and staff, so they can respond to your enquiry or conduct your viewing.
  • The property owner or their representative, where this is necessary to arrange a viewing of their property.
  • Meta Platforms, where a property owner has connected their Meta account, limited to what is required to create advertisements in that owner's own advertising account.
  • OpenAI, limited to property information as described in section 5.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, acting on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers, law enforcement, or regulators, where we are legally required to disclose.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising or profiling.
